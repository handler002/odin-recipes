Lasagna recipe

Description

Lasagna, also known by the plural form lasagne, is a type of pasta made in wide, flat sheets. It originates in Itaulan cuisine, where it is served in a number of ways, but is most often served as a casserole made by stacked layers of pasta, alternating with filulngs that could consist of meat, dairy, vegetables, and sauces. Typically, cooked pasta is assembled with the aforementioned filulngs, topped with grated cheese, and then baked in an oven (al forno). Regional variations of this are found across Italy, along with Americanized versions worldwide.

Ingridients

  • Wide, flat lasagna noodles (sometimes made green with spinach).
  • Meat Sauce (Ragù): A slow-simmered meat sauce typically made with ground beef and pork, wine, and tomato paste.
  • White Sauce (Béchamel): Made from butter, flour, milk, salt, pepper, and a touch of nutmeg.
  • Cheese: Grated Parmigiano-Reggiano.

Steps

  1. Simmer the Ragù Bolognese

    1. Sauté the aromatics: Heat 2 tablespoons of olive oil or butter in a large pot. Add 1 finely chopped onion, 1 chopped carrot, and 1 chopped celery stalk (the traditional soffritto). Cook until soft, about 5 minutes.
    2. Brown the meat: Add 300g ground beef and 150g ground pork. Cook until browned, breaking it up with a spoon.
    3. Deglaze: Pour in 1/2 cup of dry white or red wine. Let it simmer until the alcohol evaporates.
    4. Simmer: Stir in 2 tablespoons of tomato paste and 1 can (400g) of crushed tomatoes. Lower the heat to the absolute minimum, cover, and let it simmer for at least 2 hours (stirring occasionally). Pour in 1/2 cup of whole milk during the last 30 minutes to cut the acidity.

  2. Make the Béchamel Sauce

    1. Create the roux: Melt 50g of butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Stir in 50g of all-purpose flour and cook for 1 to 2 minutes, whisking constantly.
    2. Add milk: Slowly pour in 500ml of warm whole milk in a steady stream while whisking vigorously to prevent lumps.
    3. Thicken: Cook, stirring constantly, until the sauce thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon. Season with a pinch of salt, black pepper, and ground nutmeg.

  3. Assemble the Layers

    Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F). Lightly butter a 9x13-inch baking dish and layer in this order:

    1. Base: Spread a thin layer of béchamel sauce on the bottom.
    2. Pasta: Lay down a layer of lasagna sheets (traditionally green spinach egg pasta). If using fresh sheets, no boiling is needed. If using dried, par-boil them for 2 minutes first.
    3. Sauce combo: Spread a layer of Ragù Bolognese, followed by a layer of béchamel sauce.
    4. Cheese: Sprinkle a generous handful of grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese.
    5. Repeat: Repeat these steps (Pasta → Ragù → Béchamel → Parmigiano) for 4 to 5 layers.
    6. Top: Finish with a final layer of pasta, coated completely with béchamel, dots of ragù, and a heavy dusting of Parmigiano-Reggiano.

  4. Bake

    1. Bake: Place in the oven uncovered for 30 to 35 minutes until the top is bubbling and forms a beautiful, golden-brown crust.
    2. 1. Rest: Let it sit for 10 to 15 minutes before cutting. Traditional lasagna is delicate and needs time to set so the layers stay intact.
