Tteokbokki recipe

Description

Tteokbokki, topokki or simmered rice cake, is a Korean food made from small-sized garae-tteok called tteokmyeon or commonly tteokbokki-tteok. It is often paired with eomuk, boiled eggs, and scallions, and seasoned with spicy gochujang.

Ingridients

  • Rice cakes (tteok): 350g to 400g (garaetteok)
  • Fish cakes (eomuk): 150g (cut into triangles or bite-sized strips)
  • Soup stock: 2 to 3 cups of anchovy-kelp broth or water
  • Sauce:
    • 3 tbsp gochujang (Korean chili paste)
    • 1.5 to 2 tbsp raw sugar or corn syrup
    • 1 tbsp soy sauce
    • 1 to 2 tsp gochugaru (Korean chili flakes)
    • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • Aromatics & Garnish: 1 stalk green onion (chopped), 1 tsp sesame oil, and toasted sesame seeds
  • Optional Add-ins: Hard-boiled eggs, sliced onions, or instant ramen noodles (for rabokki)

Steps

  1. If your rice cakes are refrigerated or firm, soak them in warm water for 10 to 15 minutes to soften. (Fresh, soft rice cakes can go straight into the pan). Drain and set aside.
  2. Make the Broth & Sauce Base: In a wide pan or skillet, bring your anchovy-kelp stock (or water and dashida) to a gentle simmer. Whisk in the gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce, sugar (or corn syrup), and minced garlic until the paste is completely dissolved.
  3. Simmer the Rice Cakes: Add the soaked rice cakes and sliced onions (if using) into the simmering sauce. Cook on medium-high heat for about 5 to 8 minutes, stirring frequently so the starch from the rice cakes thickens the sauce and they do not stick to the bottom.
  4. Add Fish Cakes and Vegetables: Toss in the sliced fish cakes, cabbage chunks, and the white/firm parts of the green onions. Continue simmering for another 4 to 5 minutes until the vegetables are tender, the fish cakes are cooked, and the sauce turns glossy and thick.
  5. Stir in hard-boiled eggs (if using) during the final 2 minutes to warm them through. Remove from heat, drizzle with a little sesame oil, and top generously with chopped green onion greens and roasted sesame seeds.
